# Spacetime-Ripple Unified-Field Hypothesis

## Abstract

This paper proposes the Spacetime-Ripple Unified-Field Hypothesis, constructing a self-consistent conceptual physical model. Breaking the traditional physical dichotomy of "spacetime as container and fields as filling content," the theory states that spacetime and the global quantum field belong to one single entity. There is no bare spacetime detached from fields, nor independent quantum fields separated from geometric structures.

A core mechanism is put forward: propagating energy flow itself can bend spacetime, not merely rest mass. This mechanism explains gravitational deflection of massless photons, and unifies electromagnetic waves and gravitational waves as different ripple modes of the same medium. Massive particles are further understood as stable topological knot-solitons on this medium, whose winding chirality (clockwise / counterclockwise) corresponds to matter and antimatter; the uncertainty principle and the quantum measurement problem also receive a unified picture under the same ontology. The hypothesis provides a brand-new framework for dark-matter gravitational effects, matter-antimatter annihilation and pair production, and the underlying contradictions between General Relativity and Quantum Field Theory.

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Defects of Existing Theories

Classical General Relativity treats spacetime as a continuous geometric background manifold, within which all matter, electromagnetic fields and energy reside.

Standard Quantum Field Theory regards spacetime as a rigid fixed stage; all particles are field excitations defined upon this background.

Intrinsic conflicts between the two theories:
- General Relativity: spacetime is dynamic and can be curved by energy.
- Quantum Field Theory: spacetime remains constant as background.

This deep-rooted dualism makes unification of quantum-gravity difficult under traditional frameworks.

### 1.2 Core Innovation of This Hypothesis

This theory discards the assumption of a pre-existing background spacetime manifold.

**Spacetime = Quantum-Field Complex.**

There exists no empty void nor isolated quantum field. Geometry and field excitation are two observational perspectives of one fundamental reality — comparable to an ultra-low-loss elastic membrane: matter and energy are not objects added onto a spacetime background, but the membrane's own disturbance and geometric deformation.

All energy propagation across the universe is essentially ripple disturbance travelling on the Spacetime-Field-Complex medium.

## 2. Fundamental Axioms

**Axiom 1: Ontological-Integration Axiom**

The most fundamental reality of the universe is the Spacetime-Field-Complex.
- Pure empty spacetime without field excitation does not exist.
- Quantum fields separated from spacetime geometry do not exist.
- Spacetime curvature (geometric dimension) and field-energy excitation (material dimension) are dual descriptions of one physical state.

**Axiom 2: Ripple-Propagation Axiom**

All forms of energy propagation in nature are ripple transmissions of the Spacetime-Field-Complex medium.
- Gravitational wave: ripples dominated by geometric degrees of freedom.
- Electromagnetic wave / photon: ripples dominated by matter-field degrees of freedom, with weak geometric-curvature excitation attached.
- All dynamically flowing energy possesses the capability to bend local spacetime.

**Axiom 3: Mass-Free Gravitational-Coupling Axiom**

The magnitude of spacetime curvature is determined by energy-flux, not rest-mass.

Rest-mass is only a static condensed configuration of field ripples. Propagating kinetic energy and field disturbance can independently generate local spacetime curvature.

## 3. Core Deduction and Physical Interpretation

### 3.1 Essential Homology of Electromagnetic Waves and Gravitational Waves

Conventional physics treats electromagnetic waves and gravitational waves as two completely separate fundamental interactions.

Within this hypothesis, they originate from the same medium, differing only in which degrees-of-freedom are excited:
1. Gravitational wave: geometric degrees-of-freedom dominate.
2. Photon-electromagnetic wave: matter-field degrees-of-freedom dominate, accompanied by faint geometric ripples.

**Photon generation picture**: two counter-propagating spacetime ripples can be pictured as superposing and locally cancelling, driving the local disturbance function toward zero; the energy, however, cannot simply vanish, and continues outward from the superposition region as a travelling wave — this travelling wave is the photon. A photon is therefore a massless, propagating spacetime disturbance that does not form the topological knot structure discussed below.

Conclusion: The propagation of a photon itself is a faint spacetime-ripple fluctuation.

### 3.2 New Interpretation for Photon Gravitational Deflection

Classical explanation: Solar mass curves background spacetime; photons travel along spacetime geodesics and get deflected.

Dual interpretation in this theory:
1. Macro background curvature: The Sun's total enormous energy shapes large-scale spacetime curvature.
2. Intrinsic-ripple curvature: A photon is a travelling energy-ripple. Its carried energy-flux continuously distorts surrounding local spacetime during propagation.

This answers a classic puzzle: why massless photons are affected by gravity. Gravity responds to propagating energy-flux rather than rest-mass of objects.

### 3.3 Dynamic-Energy Curvature Effect

All forms of moving energy:
- Photon flux
- Kinetic-energy flow of particles
- Diffuse field oscillation

produce tiny local spacetime distortion.

Static mass = ripple condensation in stable closed-loop configuration.
Dynamic energy = open ripples spreading outward.

Both configurations are capable of generating spacetime curvature.

### 3.3.1 Ripple Superposition and the Gravitational-Superposition Mechanism

The Spacetime-Field-Complex medium obeys the wave-superposition principle: multiple sets of spacetime ripples within the same spatial region undergo interference and superposition:

1. When multiple ripples share consistent vibration phase and topological orientation, **constructive interference** occurs — the total disturbance amplitude equals the sum of each ripple's amplitude, and the local energy-flux density is correspondingly superposed and increased;
2. By Axiom 3, energy-flux density directly determines the magnitude of spacetime curvature; the superposed high energy-flux produces stronger local spacetime bending, which macroscopically manifests as gravity being mutually reinforced when multiple masses gather together;
3. Celestial bodies and galaxies are, in essence, massive numbers of densely overlapping topological solitons (massive particles); countless closed ripples undergo continuous constructive superposition within the same region, which is why large-mass celestial bodies possess extremely strong spacetime curvature;
4. If two sets of ripples are in completely opposite phase, **destructive interference** occurs — local field disturbance cancels out and energy-flux density drops, corresponding to the rapid dissipation of curvature when matter and antimatter meet and annihilate.

This superposition mechanism unifies two observational facts:
- The greater an object's mass and the more particles it contains, the stronger its gravity;
- When matter and antimatter come into contact, the gravitational source rapidly disappears and no longer produces spacetime curvature.

### 3.4 Massive Particles: The Topological-Soliton Mechanism

Unlike the photon, a particle with rest mass corresponds to a **bound** disturbance on the spacetime membrane — a topological knot-soliton.

The medium exhibits a nonlinear effect: an inward-binding dynamical tendency that counteracts the natural outward dispersion of disturbances. A simple bulge-shaped deformation, however, cannot remain stable in three-dimensional space on mathematical grounds — it would naturally disperse and vanish. For a particle to persist stably over time, it must rely on a topological knot structure so that the soliton neither disintegrates nor disperses outward; this is the geometric root of a long-lived "rest mass."

The electron is a specific case of this general mechanism: as a locally-stable self-confined excitation mode upon the Spacetime-Field-Complex medium, it cannot exist detached from the medium. Inside atoms, the atomic nucleus acts as an intense local perturbation, altering the boundary conditions of the surrounding field structure. Only certain intrinsic ripple-modes can remain stable: constructive-interference regions correspond to high electron probability, while destructive-interference creates nodes of zero probability-density. Since only self-consistent modes survive, atomic energy levels are naturally discrete. Electric charge is the intrinsic coupling property of this excitation mode, governing its interaction with other field ripples; spin represents a topological-phase feature of the mode, not mechanical rotation of a particle in space.

*Note: The above is only a physical-picture interpretation given by this hypothesis, and does not replace the established mathematical framework of quantum mechanics.*

### 3.5 Chirality of Matter and Antimatter, and Pair Production

Every massive soliton has two mirror topological winding states:
1. **Ordinary Matter**: a clockwise topological spacetime knot structure.
2. **Antimatter**: a counterclockwise mirror topological spacetime knot structure.

They possess identical mass magnitude but opposite topological sign, with charge, spin and other intrinsic properties all mirror-symmetric.

**Annihilation**: when matter and antimatter solitons meet, their mirror topological structures fully unknot and cancel; all bound mass-energy that was topologically locked is released, and the soliton disintegrates, converting into outward-propagating high-energy photon travelling waves — a complete mass-energy conversion.

**Pair production**: under high-energy conditions, a travelling-wave spacetime disturbance can undergo a topological phase transition, simultaneously exciting a pair of solitons with opposite winding chirality — matter-antimatter pair production, the time-reverse of annihilation.

### 3.6 Particle Classification

Within this framework, different elementary particles are jointly distinguished by:
- the natural/intrinsic vibration frequency of the spacetime ripple;
- wave amplitude;
- the topological knot configuration and winding number;
- topological winding chirality (distinguishing matter from antimatter).

Different combinations correspond to different particles and antiparticles in the real universe. The description in Section 3.4 — charge as a coupling property, spin as a topological-phase feature — is precisely how this classification scheme manifests for the specific case of the electron.

### 3.7 The Uncertainty Principle

Particles are not infinitesimal points but widely spread spacetime ripples; a ripple's faint influence can extend even to observable-universe scale.

Higher ripple amplitude at a given location corresponds to a higher detection probability there; lower amplitude corresponds to lower detection probability.

Forcibly compressing a ripple into an extremely small spatial region simultaneously excites a large number of sub-disturbances of differing frequency and momentum, producing momentum uncertainty — consistent in form with Heisenberg's uncertainty principle.

### 3.8 A Holistic Reading of the Measurement Problem

A soliton is understood as a globally complete topological whole. When a measurement interaction occurs, no matter or energy is superluminally transported through space; rather, the entire soliton system's global state changes synchronously.

This logic borrows from the holism of quantum entanglement: the correlation can be instantaneous, but the mechanism cannot transmit any usable information, and therefore neither violates relativistic causality nor enables superluminal communication.

## 4. Interpretation of the Dark-Matter Mechanism

Mainstream dark-matter conjectures postulate undiscovered new particles in the universe.

This hypothesis holds a different view: a large fraction of extragalactic gravitational effects originates from large-scale, diffuse, low-frequency Spacetime-Field-Complex ripples that have not condensed into physical particles. These uncondensed field-perturbations carry energy-flux and generate spacetime curvature, yet — lacking the topological knot structure required to excite electromagnetic interaction, i.e. not yet "particlized" — remain invisible to electromagnetic observation.

In other words, the physical picture behind dark matter is a **diffuse ripple mode that has not topologically closed**, rather than some new species of fundamental particle. This picture can reproduce anomalous galactic rotation curves without introducing new fundamental particles.

## 5. Re-interpretation of Matter-Antimatter Annihilation

Electron-positron annihilation does not mean matter vanishes into nothingness.

As described in Section 3.5, this is fundamentally two stable closed-loop mass configurations of opposite chirality (matter and antimatter solitons) undergoing topological unknotting, converting into outward-propagating open ripples — namely, photons.

Total energy of the Spacetime-Field-Complex is conserved. Mass is merely one stable configuration among the ripples; annihilation is only a change of configuration, with the energy always remaining within the field medium.

The root of the traditional conflict lies in the a priori division between "background spacetime" and "matter within the field." This model unifies geometry and field into one fundamental entity, eliminating background-dependence and providing a conceptual basis for quantum gravity.

Provides the underlying physical picture for photon gravitational deflection: the source of gravity is energy-flux, not restricted to rest mass.

### 6.3 Explaining Dark Matter without Adding New Fundamental Particles

Does not exclude new-particle dark-matter candidates, but offers an alternative viable explanation: large-scale field disturbance that has not yet become particlized.

### 6.4 Unifying the Nature of Waves

Gravitational waves and electromagnetic waves are unified as different ripple-modes on the same medium.

### 6.5 Accommodating Quantum Foundational Questions within the Same Ontology

The uncertainty principle and the measurement problem are usually treated as interpretive puzzles internal to quantum mechanics, independent of gravity. This hypothesis attempts to show that a particle's diffuseness (the uncertainty principle) and a soliton's holism (the measurement problem) can both be derived naturally from the same geometric picture — "a particle is a ripple/soliton" — without introducing additional assumptions.

## 8. Discussion

### 8.1 Comparison with Standard Quantum Field Theory

Standard Quantum Field Theory presupposes a spacetime manifold as a fixed background container upon which quantum fields are defined. This hypothesis holds that spacetime itself is the manifestation of the field complex.

Under low-energy conditions without strong gravity, the field-geometry coupling effect is weak, and the model degenerates to be compatible with ordinary Quantum Field Theory; existing experimental results continue to hold. Differences may only emerge at Planck-scale energy or in cosmological scenarios.

### 8.2 Origin of Spacetime Curvature

General Relativity adopts the stress-energy tensor as the source of spacetime curvature. This is an empirically verified relation, yet it does not answer why energy can deform spatial geometry.

Within this hypothesis: energy does not act upon a separate, independent "space" to bend it as a container. Energy-excitation and geometric deformation are two descriptions of one single medium-perturbation; when the complex medium generates a ripple-disturbance, energy and local spacetime-curvature emerge simultaneously, with no need for an additional, independent "acting" process.

### 8.3 Relation to Prior Geometric and Topological Theories

This hypothesis draws extensively on existing historical physical ideas, reorganizing and re-deriving from them:

- **Wheeler's Geometrodynamics** attempts to explain matter using only spacetime geometry and topology, proposing "mass without mass, charge without charge." Its limitation is that it largely remains at the classical geometric level, without fully incorporating quantum wave behavior. This hypothesis further unifies quantum ripples, topological solitons, and matter-antimatter chirality within the same spacetime ontology.
- The **Skyrme soliton model** treats baryons as topological knot-solitons on a field, gaining stability from topological conservation. The difference is that the original Skyrme soliton is built on a matter field within a background spacetime, whereas the soliton in this hypothesis is a disturbance of spacetime itself, requiring no additional background field.
- The **Wave Structure of Matter (WSM)** describes particles as the superposition of inward- and outward-travelling spherical standing waves, with the particle wave diffusing over a large spatial region. The difference is that WSM treats space as a fixed background medium, whereas in this hypothesis the disturbance is itself spacetime geometry.
- **String theory** associates different vibration modes with different particle species. The difference is that strings are objects existing within a spacetime background, whereas in this hypothesis the vibrating medium is spacetime itself.
- **Standard QFT's antiparticle mechanism** understands antiparticles as the positive/negative excitation of a quantum field. This hypothesis visualizes antiparticles concretely as mirror solitons with opposite spacetime-topological winding chirality, giving an intuitive picture from a geometric point of view.

## 9. Open Problems and Limitations

This hypothesis currently remains a conceptual picture at the level of text and logic; a rigorous mathematical formalism has not yet been established. The following are outstanding gaps:

1. **Lack of complete field equations**: The framework is currently entirely qualitative logic, without corresponding differential equations; it cannot yet perform quantitative calculations or directly reproduce precise values of physical constants or particle masses.
2. **Unsolved 3D static soliton stability dynamics**: Stability relies on the topological-knot assumption, but the detailed dynamics of knot formation, evolution, and dissolution have not been derived.
3. **Origin of the electromagnetic, strong, and weak interactions**: It remains unexplained how these three interactions arise entirely from spacetime ripples and topological structure.
4. **Matter-antimatter asymmetry (baryon asymmetry)**: The model can explain pair production and annihilation, but cannot currently explain why the universe today contains far more ordinary matter than antimatter — an important direction for future derivation.
5. **No decisive experimental predictions**: The theoretical tendencies listed in Section 7 remain qualitative and in-principle; the model cannot yet offer precise predictions, distinguishable from General Relativity and the Standard Model, that could be independently tested or falsified through astronomical observation or particle experiments.
6. **Reconciling non-locality with the locality of General Relativity**: A soliton possesses a globally non-local, holistic property (Section 3.8); how this is to be reconciled with General Relativity's locality assumption — that a point is only affected by neighboring spacetime — requires further thought.

**Future research directions**:
- Learn differential geometry and field theory, and attempt to translate this textual picture into rigorous mathematical expression.
- Search for observable signatures corresponding to this model, such as distinctive gravitational-wave waveforms or dark-matter gravitational-lensing features.
- Derive the mechanism of early-universe spacetime disturbance, attempting to explain matter-antimatter asymmetry.
- Investigate how the electromagnetic, strong, and weak interactions might be derived from spacetime topological ripples.

## 10. Conclusion

The Spacetime-Ripple Unified-Field Hypothesis abandons classical spacetime-matter dualism, proposing that field and spacetime geometry are one and the same physical reality: field equals spacetime, spacetime equals field, and energy propagation itself generates spacetime curvature.

On this basis, massive particles are understood as stable topological knot-solitons on this medium, whose winding chirality corresponds to matter and antimatter, with pair production and annihilation corresponding to topological phase transitions; the uncertainty principle and the quantum measurement problem likewise receive a unified interpretation under the same "particle-as-ripple" geometric picture.

The model unifies gravitational waves and electromagnetic waves, gives a new understanding of photon gravitational deflection, offers an alternative explanation of the dark-matter effect, attempts to resolve the conceptual conflict between General Relativity and Quantum Field Theory, and provides a self-consistent conceptual model for the direction of quantum-gravity unification.
